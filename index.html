Registration

Registration Fees

Entry fee for the I Funchal Chess Open. The amount depends on the date the payment is made.

Paid by Fee
26 October 50 €
26 November 60 €
After 26 November 70 €

Players resident in the Madeira region receive a 30% discount on the entry fee.

GMs, IMs and players rated above 2300 are exempt from the entry fee.

How to Register

1

Check your fee

Find the fee that applies to you, taking into account the payment date, the Madeira residency discount, and the exemption for GMs, IMs and players rated above 2300.

2

Pay the entry fee

Make the payment and keep the proof. Payment details (IBAN and accepted methods) will be published here shortly.

3

Send your registration email

Email festivaldexadrezdofunchal@gmail.com with your name, your FIDE ID and the proof of payment. Your registration is confirmed only once all three are received.

Required Information

  • Full name
  • FIDE ID
  • Proof of payment
  • Federation and rating (if applicable)
  • Contact email address

Send everything in a single email to festivaldexadrezdofunchal@gmail.com. The organisation reserves the right of admission.
